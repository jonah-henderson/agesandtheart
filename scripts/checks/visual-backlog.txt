# ══ 10. THE VOLCANIC CONES HAVE NEVER BEEN SEEN ══════════════════════════════ Built 2026-09-06 ══
# Step one of the volcanoes: the recipe entry and the shape. The cones generate; nothing is in them yet.
#
# **SHAPE IS JUDGED BY EYE, NOT BY A CHECK** (Jonah, 2026-09-06). These are meant to be organic and noisy
# rather than cookie-cutter, so pinning them with assertions buys flaky tests and an incentive to make the
# generation more regular than it should be. A shape check written for this was deleted the same day.
#
/age compose volc 11 landmass=hills sea=water rock=solid sky=plain features.grows=agesandtheart:volcano
/age tp volc
#
# The lava tubes and the projectiles are in as of 2026-09-06. A vent under 16 connected blocks only seeps;
# from there the bombs ramp to a full 64-block mass, creeper-strength up to well past TNT.
#
# **NOTHING HERE HAS BEEN SEEN.** Volcanoes sit roughly a kilometre apart, so a small generated area may
# hold none at all -- fly, or generate wide, before concluding they are missing.
#
# WHAT A WALK HAS TO SETTLE, all of it dial-turning rather than pass/fail:
#   - **THAT THE VENTS ARE FOUND AT ALL.** `VolcanoVents` looks for a column high up with higher ground on
#     all four sides, which is the one piece of this most likely to be silently wrong -- if the caldera is
#     wider or shallower than `RIM_REACH`/`RIM_CLIMB` expect, it finds nothing and the crater sits empty.
#     An empty caldera is the symptom.
#   - That the tubes well lava up into the caldera and STOP at the rim rather than running down the flank.
#     The pool is capped at the mass's own height and 24 blocks from it; a breached rim should drain, and
#     whether that is a bug or a feature is a judgement.
#   - That PLUGGING works: put a solid block over a tube and the welling should stop. Lava over it must
#     NOT count as a plug, or a volcano silences itself on its own output.
#   - That mining the tubes out is a real, findable act -- can you tell where they are once the caldera has
#     filled with lava?
#   - **THE BOMBS, WHICH ARE THE WHOLE HAZARD AND ARE ENTIRELY UNSEEN.** They arc, they are drawn as a
#     magma block, they explode on hitting a block or a player and pool a little lava in the crater. Are
#     they DODGEABLE -- can you see one coming and move? That is the counterplay, and if the arc is too
#     flat or too fast there is none.
#   - That a bomb landing IN lava is inert. This is the bound on the whole mechanic: flooded ground stops
#     accumulating, so a volcano deepens its pool and then stops enlarging it. If it still explodes there,
#     an Age drifts toward being made of lava.
#   - That 16 connected tubes is the right place for the threshold, and that mining back under it really
#     does silence a volcano without having to dig out every block.
#   - Whether a full 64-mass at explosion strength 7 is exciting or merely unplayable near a base.
#   - **FIRE SPREAD.** A hot Age with rain suppressed and lava raining into it could be miserable in a way
#     only a walk shows.
#   - Do they read as VOLCANOES from a distance, or as ordinary hills with a dent? Base radius 56, height
#     96, caldera radius 14 and 18 deep are all first guesses.
#   - Does the caldera look like somewhere you would climb into? It is where the tubes will go, so it has
#     to be enterable and worth entering.
#   - Are they RARE ENOUGH TO BE A LANDMARK and common enough to find? Roughly a kilometre between
#     candidates with patch clustering, so volcanic country should come in fields with quiet ground
#     between rather than an even sprinkle.
#   - Do they sit ON the landform convincingly? They are a layer unioned over the Age's own ground, so try
#     them over `cliffs` and `alps` as well as `hills` -- a cone growing out of a cliff face is the case
#     most likely to look wrong.
#   - Does the blend turn two overlapping cones into one massif, or leave a crease?

# ══ 9. TEMPERSTONE'S THREE BANDS HAVE NEVER BEEN SEEN ════════════════════════ Built 2026-09-06 ══
# The whole design is that ONE FORMATION TEACHES THE RULE: stone too close to lava comes out scorched and
# worthless, stone at the right remove is temperstone, stone beyond that never changed. If a player cannot
# read that off the ground without being told, the material has no reason to exist in this shape.
#
# The gate is checked offline (`EarlyGameRareMaterialsCheck`, 5 tests) and the `forbidden` fence is checked
# on a server (`scripts/checks/temperstone.txt` — all three exploit compositions were refused). Neither can
# see a placed block: there is no block census command, and `/age probe` reads the generator rather than
# what decoration laid down.
#
# Composed, not written: no word reaches a numeric temperature span yet, so the gate sentence is still open.
/age compose baked 11 landmass=hills sea=lava rock=solid sky=plain climate.temperature=0.6..0.9
/age tp baked
#
# WHAT A WALK HAS TO SETTLE:
#   - **THAT THE FOUR BANDS ARE LEGIBLE AS BANDS.** Scorched at 1, temperstone at 2-3, raw at 4-5, ordinary
#     ground beyond. THIS IS THE WHOLE FEATURE: "this far is too far, this close is too close, this
#     distance gets you the good stuff." If the eye cannot separate them at the lava's edge, the
#     thicknesses are wrong -- and smooth basalt / netherrack / tuff are standing in for all three, so
#     judge the SHAPE and not the colour.
#   - Whether one lava sea yields far too much temperstone. The bands are a skin on the contact area,
#     which for a sea is large.
#   - That the scorched band reads as SPOILED rather than as a second reward. It is the toll.
#   - **THAT THE PRACTICE WORKS, which is the half the formation only teaches.** Mine raw temperstone from
#     a blob (14 a chunk, size 9 -- deliberately common), carry it to lava, place it at the right remove,
#     and wait for the random tick. It should temper. Place it too close and it should spoil. If this is
#     tedious rather than satisfying, the tick rate or the band widths are the dials.
#   - **THE CLIMBERS.** Any temperstone face, from any side, wearing them. Vanilla has no free-climb, so
#     there is nothing to compare it to -- does it feel like climbing, or like sticking to a wall?
#     Stopping should HOLD YOU IN PLACE, forward or jump should rise, sneak should descend. The thing to
#     try to break is letting go of everything halfway up.
#   - That the boot-slot trade against the skates is a real choice and not a dominated one.
#   - **THE PISTON, WHICH IS UNVERIFIED ON BOTH LOADERS AND CANNOT BE CHECKED HEADLESSLY** -- it needs a
#     piston to actually fire. A run of temperstone must move as ONE, and must NOT drag anything else
#     (that would be a slime block). **Walk it on Fabric AND NeoForge**: the two halves are entirely
#     different code. Fabric goes through `PistonStructureResolverMixin`; NeoForge through the two plain
#     methods on `TemperstoneBlock`, which are overrides only at runtime and would fail silently if the
#     signature ever drifts.
#   - NOT BUILT AND DELIBERATELY: the underside. Hanging from a temperstone roof was cut with ceiling
#     walking rather than shipped as its cheap half -- design 7.4 carries the argument.
#   - NOT BUILT: scorched temperstone currently grinds into nothing at all, the station being deferred.

# ══ 8. SKY POLISH ══════════════════════════════════════ OPEN 2026-07-30, DEFERRED BY JONAH ══
# Park these until the main mod has more headway. Neither is a bug; both are "it works, and it should be
# better". Recorded because the leads are fresh.
#
# 8a. THE SPIRE SHOULD BE PERMANENTLY DUSK, WITH ITS STARS ALWAYS OUT.
# It has no sun, so a day/night cycle buys it nothing. Wanted: stars visible at ALL times, light EVEN
# across the cycle at roughly dusk intensity, and time still PROGRESSING so the stars rotate. Today it is
# on the overworld's clock and goes properly dark and properly bright.
# THE LEAD, and the one real unknown: `star_angle` and `star_brightness` come from the SAME timeline
# (`#minecraft:in_overworld`), so rotation and fading cannot be separated by dropping the timeline — that
# would freeze the stars, which is the opposite of what is wanted. Settle first whether a constant
# `EnvironmentAttributeMap` entry on `age_spire.json` OVERRIDES a timeline track or is merely another layer
# under it (`EnvironmentAttributeSystem.bakeLayerSampler`). If it overrides, this is data only: pin
# `star_brightness` and the light attributes and leave `star_angle` to the timeline. If it does not, the
# painter has to stop reading vanilla's `starBrightness` for an Age that says so — which probably means
# `StarField` carrying its own constant brightness and `StarReveal` going away rather than being widened.
#
# 8b. THE WARM/COOL STAR AXIS IS TOO SUBTLE TO SEE.
# `WARM_STAR` (0.95, 0.87, 0.76) and `COOL_STAR` (0.78, 0.85, 1.0) are both nearly white, so the variation
# reads as noise rather than as colour. Push both toward saturation.
# WORTH KNOWING BEFORE TURNING THAT DIAL: the tint is `WARM.lerp(COOL, random.nextFloat())`, and a uniform
# draw puts most stars in the MIDDLE of the axis — neutral — however far apart the ends are. Biasing the
# draw toward the ends will do at least as much as widening it, and probably more.
