# ══ 10. THE VOLCANOES ══════════════════════════════ Rebuilt 2026-09-09, NOTHING WALKED ══
# Jonah's design, in two halves. **Lava is terrain**: a caldera arrives completely and uniformly flooded
# because the Age's own shape filled it, so nothing at runtime has a crater to race. And a tube no longer
# computes a pool at all — it lays one block overhead if there is room, then turns the NEAREST RUNNING LAVA
# ITS OWN POOL REACHES into a source. Vanilla's fluid only ever puts flowing lava where lava could go, so
# containment is inherited rather than worked out.
#
# Throwing is a PROBABILITY rather than a threshold: a lone tube about every two minutes, a crowded one on
# every visit, so a mass scales danger twice over — more visits, and each likelier.
#
/age compose volc 11 landmass=hills sea=water rock=solid sky=plain features.grows=agesandtheart:volcano
/age tp volc
#
# **THE OFFLINE HALF IS ALREADY MEASURED.** `VolcanoLakesCheck` holds the two things that fail silently —
# every block of lava met by lava or rock, and every crater holding some — and prints a cross-section: on
# seed 11's nearest cone, floor flat at y=95, lava level at y=110, rim 112 to 118. `LavaTubesCheck` holds
# the cadence: 2,395 ticks between a lone tube's shots, 144 for a seam of twelve, 13 for a caldera vent.
# Driven on a real server: 4,693 lava over two chunk columns of the crater, a 267-block vent under it,
# bombs in the air, and a single tube in a sealed pit growing 4 -> 66 -> 173 -> 254 and levelling at 285,
# which is its reach-8 ball and nothing more.
#
# WHAT THIS WALK HAS TO SETTLE — all of it, because none of it has been seen:
#   - **IS THE CALDERA WHAT YOU WANTED?** The lava stands SIX blocks under the rim, about fifteen deep.
#   - **THE INNER WALLS ARE SMOOTHER THAN THE FLANKS**, because the crater is cut clean AFTER the mountain
#     is roughened. That is what buys a level lake. Does it read as a collapse scarp, or as a cookie
#     cutter? The rim, the flank and the outline all keep every bit of their noise.
#   - **AND DOES IT SPILL?** The summit ring is eaten into by a wandering slab, so a rim comes out notched
#     where the noise chose and lava runs over the lip and down the flank — permanently, since generation
#     laid sources and a source does not empty. **Three craters in six spill and three do not**, which is
#     the point: a guaranteed channel through every rim was tried first and read as cookie-cutter.
#     Measured at 69 blocks of lava facing open air on the nearest cone, lowest at y=107 against a surface
#     of 110. MOUNTAIN_EROSION is the dial — more means more craters spill and deeper notches.
#   - **DOES A VENT UNDER SIX BLOCKS OF LAVA STILL READ AS A VENT?** It is a cone rising through the lake
#     now, 267 tubes against 117, which is a shot every five ticks — several in the air at once. It stops
#     three blocks under the surface so it always erupts from under its own lava. Too much?
#   - **THE SMALL CRATERS, WHICH ARE NEW.** A second instanced layer at a fifth the scale, on 120-block
#     cells: spatter cones, a shallow maar, a pond with barely a lip. Each holds a level lake of its own
#     and `VolcanoVents` seats a vent in it exactly as it does a caldera, so they shoot too — weakly, being
#     small. Measured over a square kilometre: 618 of 66,049 columns hold lava in NINE bodies. Is that the
#     right pocking, and do they read as volcanic rather than as puddles?
#   - **THE MAGMA CHAMBERS, ALSO NEW, AND THE ONE THING HERE WITH TWO FACES.** An ellipsoid cavity with a
#     level pool in its bottom, cut from the rock at generation, about one per 260 blocks square and spread
#     down the rock from y=14 to y=-50. Where a carver opens one it is a lava lake in a cavern with
#     headroom; where the rock is whole it is a hazard that floods the tunnel the moment you break in.
#     Nothing decides which — caves and chambers are scattered independently. **Walk a cave and also dig a
#     tunnel**, because those are two different findings.
#   - **AND THE FALLS**, which is what the chambers were for: does a cave coming in above a chamber give
#     you a lavafall into it?
#   - **PLACE A TUBE BY HAND.** Breaking the rock over one is a neighbour change, so it starts welling AT
#     ONCE rather than on whatever visit vanilla gets round to — which is what a walk found when a tube
#     laid on flat ground appeared to do nothing for a minute. Driven at the default tick rate: five blocks
#     of lava in the first second, then 25, 40, and 265 after four minutes, level there because that is its
#     reach. Is the SUDDEN part sudden enough and the rest slow enough not to be a faucet?
#   - **A LONE TUBE THROWS EVERY TWO MINUTES**, which is new: there is no minimum mass any more. In a mine
#     that is a rare, weak, genuinely surprising shot. Exciting or annoying?
#   - **A SEAM, AND THEN A NEST at one chunk in four.** Seams of 12 at 7 a chunk, all kept eight blocks
#     under the heightmap so the rock is their enclosure. Thirty tubes each hardening within eight blocks
#     of itself is a lake filling the space you made, with shots starting up in it. Exciting, or does it
#     end the mine? Findable while mining ordinarily? They emit light 6, and a tube in a WALL or CEILING is
#     plugged and only glows while one in a FLOOR is live.
#   - **IS IT TOO GOOD A LAVA SOURCE?** Deliberately renewable, deliberately constrained by the danger:
#     roughly a bucket every two seconds at a nest, against vanilla dripstone at one per eight with no
#     limit. And you have to leave it uncovered and erupting, which is the trade.
#   - **NOTHING EXPOSED ON THE SURFACE OUTSIDE A CRATER.** Stray vents in ordinary hill basins are gone — a
#     vent is seated where the Age's lava already stands and nowhere else. Confirm you find no bare tubes
#     on open hillsides.
#   - **THE DROPS**, which Jonah was mid-check on: netherite-only and silk-touch-only, and all four blocks
#     had been dropping NOTHING with any tool until the mineable/pickaxe entry was added.
#   - Does the blend turn two overlapping skirts into one massif, or leave a crease?
#   - LOW PRIORITY, DEFERRED BY JONAH: that PLUGGING works — a solid block over a tube stops the welling,
#     and lava over it does NOT count. It is the only control surface left, so it matters more than it did:
#     a tube you cover produces nothing and throws nothing.

# ══ 9. TEMPERSTONE'S THREE BANDS ═══════════════════ THREE FIXES TO RE-READ ══
/age compose baked 11 landmass=hills sea=lava rock=solid sky=plain climate.temperature=0.6..0.9
/age tp baked
#
#   - **TEMPERED STONE SPOILS NOW IF THE HEAT REACHES IT.** Only RAW stone answered to heat, so the
#     finished material was immune to the hazard it was made in — and the bands are laid at generation
#     against the lava as it stood then, so a settling lava sea left temperstone sitting against lava for
#     ever. Look along a shore for tempered stone touching lava: it should go scorched. Moving a block too
#     close by hand should spoil it too. It only ever goes FORWARD — taking the heat away does not
#     un-temper anything.
#   - **NATURAL FORMATIONS ONLY COOK BELOW y=32.** A lava sea's rim yielded enough for the armour without
#     trying, so finding it is a descent now. **A gate on GENERATION and deliberately not on the rule** —
#     carrying raw stone to ANY lava still works, and is meant to. `NOTHING_ABOVE` is the dial; judge
#     whether a lava sea still yields enough once you go down to its floor.
#   - **THE BAKING WAVE IS HALF AS FAST** (`SPREAD_DELAY` 10 -> 20). It was right in kind and a little
#     quick to read as a process.

# ══ 8. SKY POLISH ══════════════════════════════════ DEFERRED BY JONAH; leads kept fresh ══
# Park these until the main mod has more headway. Neither is a bug; both are "it works, and it should be
# better".
#
# 8a. THE SPIRE SHOULD BE PERMANENTLY DUSK, WITH ITS STARS ALWAYS OUT.
# It has no sun, so a day/night cycle buys it nothing. Wanted: stars visible at ALL times, light EVEN
# across the cycle at roughly dusk intensity, and time still PROGRESSING so the stars rotate. Today it is
# on the overworld's clock and goes properly dark and properly bright.
# THE LEAD, and the one real unknown: `star_angle` and `star_brightness` come from the SAME timeline
# (`#minecraft:in_overworld`), so rotation and fading cannot be separated by dropping the timeline — that
# would freeze the stars, which is the opposite of what is wanted. Settle first whether a constant
# `EnvironmentAttributeMap` entry on `age_spire.json` OVERRIDES a timeline track or is merely another layer
# under it (`EnvironmentAttributeSystem.bakeLayerSampler`). If it overrides, this is data only: pin
# `star_brightness` and the light attributes and leave `star_angle` to the timeline. If it does not, the
# painter has to stop reading vanilla's `starBrightness` for an Age that says so — which probably means
# `StarField` carrying its own constant brightness and `StarReveal` going away rather than being widened.
#
# 8b. THE WARM/COOL STAR AXIS IS TOO SUBTLE TO SEE.
# `WARM_STAR` (0.95, 0.87, 0.76) and `COOL_STAR` (0.78, 0.85, 1.0) are both nearly white, so the variation
# reads as noise rather than as colour. Push both toward saturation.
# WORTH KNOWING BEFORE TURNING THAT DIAL: the tint is `WARM.lerp(COOL, random.nextFloat())`, and a uniform
# draw puts most stars in the MIDDLE of the axis — neutral — however far apart the ends are. Biasing the
# draw toward the ends will do at least as much as widening it, and probably more.
