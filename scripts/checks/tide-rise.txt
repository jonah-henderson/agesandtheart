# The tide's rise, driven where no player stands to see it.
# Run with: scripts/drive-server.sh scripts/checks/tide-rise.txt
#
# The rise puts the sea back wherever generation laid it and runs on from there a block a pass, as far as
# eight blocks past it, only ever beside the sea and only where a cell would hold the water. Three things are
# built in an ocean Age whose high water is y=62, the tide is taken down to low and back up to high, and each
# is counted by replacing its sources with glass:
#   - a stone roof on the sea, which high water must come back in under;
#   - an L-shaped channel a block wide, cut into a block of stone and open to the sea at one end;
#   - a glass room sealed in the shallows and pumped dry, which must stay dry although generation laid sea
#     inside it.
#
# MEASURED 2026-09-30: under the roof y=60..62 full (26, 27, 27 of 27); the channel full on every layer (12 of
# 12); the room empty. On the coast Age (`age polar sun tidal moon` at seed 7, sea to x=84 along z=320) a
# trench dug inland from x=85 filled with sources to x=91 at y=62 and x=89 at y=61 — eight past where
# generation's sea stops on each layer — and drained at low.
#
# Never snapshot a layer by cloning it up the same columns: that lifts each column's top out of the band and
# the ebb passes it over. Counting here is done in place, and a snapshot belongs in columns the tide never
# works.
