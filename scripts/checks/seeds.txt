# Does an Age's seed reach its terrain? (two seeds, one recipe otherwise)
age compose seedA 111 landmass=overworld sea=water
age compose seedB 999 landmass=overworld sea=water
age compare seedA seedB 1

# And the same question of a landform of ours, which builds its fields from the recipe's seed directly.
age compose hillsA 111 landmass=gentle sea=water
age compose hillsB 999 landmass=gentle sea=water
age compare hillsA hillsB 1

# And the invariant the whole recipe model rests on: the same seed builds the same world, twice.
age compose sameA 4242 landmass=overworld sea=water
age compose sameB 4242 landmass=overworld sea=water
age compare sameA sameB 1
