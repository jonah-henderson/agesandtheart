# The two landforms added for Phase 7 step 4 — do they generate, and does a book reach them?
# Run with: scripts/drive-server.sh scripts/checks/new-landforms.txt
#
# Both are exercised twice over: once composed by hand, which says the field tree and the biome layer
# agree about them, and once written as a sentence, which says the minted page actually resolves to the
# preset. The second is the half a unit check cannot see, `DerivedWords.designs` minting from an enum
# that a datapack then has to carry a tag profile for.

# The flat world. What to read: `flatlands` claims no waterline at all, so the biomes have to come out as
# dry inland country rather than as one endless coast — see `Grounding.hasSea`.
age compose plain 7 landmass=flatlands rock=solid
age gen plain
age biomes plain

# The same with caves under it, which is what the demo preset writes: a level world with somewhere to go.
age compose plaincaves 7 landmass=flatlands rock=caves
age gen plaincaves

# And with the halls, since a plain at y=64 leaves room for exactly one storey and the arithmetic that
# decides that is `GreatHalls.storeysBetween` against `Terrain.undergroundCeiling`.
age compose plainhalls 7 landmass=flatlands rock=caves underground=great_halls
age gen plainhalls

# The lone island at its ordinary size. What to read: one island at the origin, ocean biomes over the sea
# round it, and a sandy shore where the two meet.
age compose alone 7 landmass=isle sea=water rock=caves
age gen alone
age biomes alone

# The two ends of its size ladder, which reaches further than the archipelago's at both.
age compose alonesmall 7 landmass=isle[size=-1.0..-0.9] sea=water
age gen alonesmall
age compose alonelarge 7 landmass=isle[size=0.9..1.0] sea=water
age gen alonelarge

# Written rather than composed: the minted pages have to resolve to the presets, and the readout has to
# say them back.
age write writtenplain 7 age flatlands landmass
age write writtenisle 7 age isle landmass
age gen writtenplain
age gen writtenisle

# Cost against a same-session reference, since both are meant to be cheap — one is a slab and the other
# is two noise samples a column.
age compose reference 7 landmass=hills sea=water rock=caves
age gen reference
age bench reference 4
age bench plaincaves 4
age bench alone 4
