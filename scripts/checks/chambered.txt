# The D'ni environment, as the canonical formula spells it — Phase 7 step 4.
# Run with: scripts/drive-server.sh scripts/checks/chambered.txt
#
# The formula is `subterranean landmass` + `colossal chambered underground` + a water sea + the algae. The algae
# does not exist yet, so what this drives is the first three: does the rock generate, does it read as
# roofed without anybody writing `sealed`, and is there a chamber with water in it at the origin.

# The bare rock: no surface anywhere, and the caves are the underground's rather than the landform's.
age compose rock 7 landmass=solid underground=none
age gen rock

# The same with vanilla's own caves through the whole build height, which is the ordinary way to write it.
age compose warren 7 landmass=solid sea=water underground=noise_caves
age gen warren
age biomes warren

# And chambered, at the size the formula asks for. What to read: the arrival should be UNDER the roof
# rather than on top of it, which is `Ages.footingIn` taking its roofed branch — and that branch is only
# taken because the landform declares it roofs the world, since nothing here says `sealed`.
age compose vaults 7 landmass=solid sea=water underground=chambered[size=1.0..1.0]
age gen vaults
age biomes vaults

# Written rather than composed: the whole formula as a sentence, which is what a player would lay.
age write dni 7 age subterranean landmass colossal chambered underground water sea
age gen dni

# The same chambers under an ordinary surface world, since the point of putting them on the underground
# aspect rather than in a landform is that they go anywhere with room for them.
age compose undercanyon 7 landmass=canyon sea=water underground=chambered
age gen undercanyon

# Cost against a same-session reference.
age compose reference 7 landmass=hills sea=water rock=caves
age gen reference
age bench reference 4
age bench warren 4
age bench vaults 4

# The algae, which is the fourth criterion and the last of step 4. What to read: the two Ages below are
# the same world bar the algae, so `/age compare` counts the mats it grew -- and they can only have gone
# on the face of the vault's lake, since a `solid` Age has no open sky in it anywhere.
age compose bare 7 landmass=solid sea=water underground=chambered[size=1.0..1.0]
age compose grown 7 landmass=solid sea=water underground=chambered[size=1.0..1.0] features.grows=agesandtheart:algae
age gen bare
age gen grown
age compare bare grown 3

# And the whole formula as a sentence, which is what a player would lay.
age write dnifull 7 age subterranean landmass colossal chambered underground water sea algae features
age gen dnifull
