# Set-valued slots: do territories generate, and does the custom surface rule survive a real server?
# Run with: scripts/drive-server.sh scripts/checks/regions.txt
#
# The load-bearing risk here is registration. RegionRule is a custom SurfaceRules.RuleSource reached
# through an access widener, and RegionBiomeSource a custom BiomeSource; if either fails to register or
# the widened access is missing at runtime, generation throws rather than looking wrong. So the first
# thing to read is simply whether these finish.

# One dressing over two landforms: the shape splits, the painting does not.
age compose ground 7 landform=hills,pillars medium=sea subsurface=solid dressing=bare_rock sky=plain
age gen ground

# Two dressings over one landform: the painting splits, the shape does not. This is the one that
# exercises RegionRule — expect grass in some territories and bare stone in others.
age compose cover 7 landform=hills medium=sea subsurface=solid dressing=verdant,bare_rock sky=plain
age gen cover

# Both at once, so all three consumers of a territory map are live together.
age compose bothways 7 landform=hills,pillars medium=sea subsurface=caves dressing=verdant,overworld sky=storm
age gen bothways
age bench bothways 4

# Same sentence, same seed, twice. Regions must not have cost us reproducibility.
age compose repeatA 99 landform=hills,pillars medium=sea dressing=verdant,bare_rock
age compose repeatB 99 landform=hills,pillars medium=sea dressing=verdant,bare_rock
age compare repeatA repeatB 2

# Every recipe should print its territory character, and paste back into /age compose.
age list

# --- Medium and subsurface, the last two positional slots ---------------------------------------
# An ocean of water meeting an ocean of lava at the same level, with no barrier between them. This is
# the one that has no vanilla equivalent at all, so it is the one worth walking to.
age compose twoseas 11 landform=hills medium=sea,lava subsurface=solid dressing=bare_rock sky=plain
age gen twoseas

# Caves in some territories and solid rock in others. Carvers are chosen per chunk rather than per
# column, so expect the seam here to follow chunk edges — that is known and accepted.
age compose halfhollow 11 landform=hills medium=sea subsurface=caves,solid dressing=overworld sky=plain
age gen halfhollow
age bench halfhollow 4

# Everything set-valued at once: four slots dividing the same world, only the sky undivided.
age compose everything 11 landform=hills,pillars medium=sea,lava subsurface=caves,solid dressing=verdant,overworld sky=storm
age gen everything
age compare everything everything 1
age list
