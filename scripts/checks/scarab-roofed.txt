# Nesting is the ground's half alone (design 7.1.2, Jonah 2026-10-01): warm by biome or by `#gives_heat`
# nearby, and open to the sky or lit. Two sealed stone rooms in the overworld, under plains so the climate
# is not warm: mud in the middle on magma, sand around it, torches on the sand and torchflowers at one end.
# The second room is the same with no magma, and should never hold a nest. Two scarabs go into each.
#
# Read the two `fill ... replace agesandtheart:scarab_nest` lines: the warm room's should fill one or more,
# the cold room's none.
#
