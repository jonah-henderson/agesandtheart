# The tide's flood and ebb, driven where no player stands to see them.
# Run with: scripts/drive-server.sh scripts/checks/tide.txt
#
# An ocean Age whose sea stands at y=62, which is high water: the tide only falls from the sea the world was
# generated around, so mid is 61 and low 60. `/age tide pass` runs one whole pass of the tide over the chunks
# around it, as a tick does around a player, and a few ticks between passes let vanilla's water move. Each stage
# is counted on its own strip of sea by replacing its water with glass, so one count never disturbs the next. A
# stone roof laid on the sea covers water the ebb has to follow in to drain.
#
# MEASURED 2026-09-30: high keeps y=62 full (240 of 240); mid leaves y=62 empty and y=61 full; low leaves y=62
# and y=61 empty and y=60 full. Under the roof, low leaves y=62 and y=61 empty.
age write tidetest 7 age ocean biomes
execute in agesandtheart:tidetest run forceload add -48 -48 63 63
execute in agesandtheart:tidetest run fill 30 63 0 34 63 10 minecraft:stone
execute in agesandtheart:tidetest run age tide high
execute in agesandtheart:tidetest positioned 8 80 8 run age tide pass
